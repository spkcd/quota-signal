# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

macOS

## Stack

SwiftUI and AppKit. Assumption for the native floating panel.

## Users

People who use one or more Claude accounts and want to see how much of their weekly allowance remains while working.

## Product Purpose

Compare weekly usage by account, see each scheduled reset, and check the availability of a free reset.

## Operating Context

A small persistent floating panel across normal desktop Spaces and eligible full-screen Spaces, with a menu bar control to bring it back.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Use Claude Code's documented status line for automatic weekly readings. Allow manually recorded readings and free reset offers from Claude Settings. Usage history consists of observed readings during the current cycle.
